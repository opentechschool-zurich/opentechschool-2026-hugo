We are a friendly group of tech enthausiast that meet regularly to work on projects, discuss technologies and help each other.

We organize events to learn, collaborate and meet other learners. If you interested in tech, would like to try out programming or if you need help with your project, feel free to join the community.

## The Co-Learning group

Each Tuesday, the OpenTechSchool Co-Learning groups meet in the [Liip Arena] at 19:00.  We make our first steps in programming or improve our coding skills.

Currently we have two Co-Learning groups:

- _Learn programming_ with Python, Javascript, Java, C(++) or …
- _Practice your programming skills_ with Python, Java, Javascript, C(++) or …

Both groups meet at the same time in the same place.

[Here](/about/#the-co-learnings), you can learn more about [our Co-Learning group](/about/#the-co-learnings).
