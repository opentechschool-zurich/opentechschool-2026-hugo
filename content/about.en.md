OpenTechSchool was a worldwide community initiative offering free programming workshops and meetups to technology enthusiasts of all genders, backgrounds, and experience levels. OTS’ main goal is to create a friendly learning environment where no one feels shy about asking any question. Everyone is invited to participate, whether as a coach or a learner, and get in contact to organize new OTS events.
