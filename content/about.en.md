OpenTechSchool is (or rather aimed to be) a worldwide community initiative offering free programming workshops and meetups to technology enthusiasts of all genders, backgrounds, and experience levels. OTS’ main goal is to create a friendly learning environment where no one feels shy about asking any question. Everyone is invited to participate, whether as a coach or a learner, and get in contact to organize new OTS events.

Currently, two chapters are active: [Leipzig](https://www.meetup.com/opentechschool-leipzig/) and Zurich.


## History

The [OpenTechSchool](https://opentechschool.org/) was born in April 2012 in Berlin.

Late Summer 2013, Robert Lehman came from Berlin to Zurich for an internship at Google, and setup the local chapter in Zurich.

We started rightaway proposing workshops for Python and Git, with the plan that had been created in Berlin.

After a couple of years, the workshop became less regular, but in September 2015 we started with a C++ learning group: a dozen of brave learners met weekly for about two years and went through each chapter in _The C++ Programming Language_ by Bjarne Stroustrup.

{{< figure src="/images/ots-cpp.webp" alt="My image" class="sponsors-logo" >}}

In December 2015 we had our last "Python for beginners" workshop, that was followed by a first "Continous Learning Group" for those who wanted to learn more.

Starting from May 2016 we started a Python learning group, in parallel to the C++ one, at the same time and place.

Since May 2019, we don't separate the learning groups by language anymore, but _simply_ publish two learning groups: one for beginners, one for intermediates (the reasoning being: beginners often need the word _beginners_ in the title to dare joining, but we need skilled in the room, and make sure that
